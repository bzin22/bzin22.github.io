## Reframe Innovations

**Founder · January 2026–Present**

At Reframe, we built procurement automation for hardware teams, connecting bill-of-materials planning, supplier communication, sourcing, and purchasing workflows. The application uses TypeScript, Supabase, TanStack Query for server state, Jotai for UI state, and Trigger.dev for Gmail synchronization and background workflows.

We evaluated negotiation behavior by labeling supplier conversations, documenting effective and ineffective tactics, and constructing preference pairs. We used these pairs to fine-tune gpt-oss with direct preference optimization through Unsloth.

## Meta

**Product Design Engineer Intern · June 2021–August 2021**

At Meta, I designed an experimental privacy slider for Oculus Quest 2, including plastic enclosures, injection-molding considerations, and tolerance stackup analyses. I conducted ergonomics research with more than 20 participants and presented prototype findings to industrial design teams, enabling a 20% reduction in the privacy switch's footprint. I also ran thermal and humidity tests, collecting more than 20,000 telemetry data points for environmental modeling.

## Barclays

**Summer Analyst · June 2020–August 2020**

At Barclays, I evaluated credit line reduction strategies across Barclaycard portfolios using model analytics, regulatory standards, and commercial exclusion policies. I analyzed operational and policy differences across the U.S., Germany, U.K., and Small Business card divisions, and refined targeting parameters for U.S. credit reduction strategies, reducing monthly net credit losses by $13.4 million in July 2020.

## BAE Systems

**Mechanical Engineering Intern · August 2019–December 2019**

At BAE Systems, I designed a hardware interface connecting APKWS electronics with a Honeywell inertial measurement unit, using Creo Sheetmetal for the flex circuit layout. I ran structural and buckling simulations in ANSYS and developed computational fluid dynamics models in STAR-CCM+ to evaluate aerodynamic profiles and support production cost reduction.
