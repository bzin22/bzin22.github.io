I am a researcher interested in applying machine learning and computational modeling to understand global manufacturing and industrial systems. I am particularly interested in predictive methods, reinforcement learning, and computational networks for studying how firms, production networks, and markets respond to technological, economic, and geopolitical change.

## Research interests

- Supply-chain risk and financial markets
- Predictive methods, including multi-agent models and reinforcement learning
- Industrial systems, production networks, and computational modeling

I hold a B.S. in Mechanical Engineering with a minor in Business from Cornell University.
